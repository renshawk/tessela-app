### **Tessella v1.17— First Public Release**

Tessella is a smarter window manager for macOS, built to make organizing your workspace fast, visual, and effortless.

This first public release introduces two core features designed to improve everyday productivity:

### Highlights
Smart Grid window snapping
Hover over the native green Zoom button to open a visual layout grid, then click and drag to instantly resize and position windows exactly where you want them Or select one of your custom Positions 


### Dock window previews
Hover over an app in the Dock to see thumbnail previews of its open windows, then click the one you want to bring it forward.

### Fast, lightweight experience
Tessella stays out of the way until you need it, helping you manage windows without interrupting your workflow.

Compatibility
macOS 10.15 or later
Supports both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs
Why Tessella
macOS is beautiful, but window management has often felt limited for power users. Tessella brings together visual window layouts and Dock-level window previews in a single polished utility, making it easier to stay organized and focused throughout the day.

Thanks for trying Tessella — this is the first public release, and more improvements are on the way.
